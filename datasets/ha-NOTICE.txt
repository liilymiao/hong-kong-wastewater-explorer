Hospital Authority blood-culture antimicrobial non-susceptibility

Source: Centre for Health Protection / Hospital Authority, Hong Kong SAR Government.
Report release: 30 January 2026; tables generated December 2025.
Original: https://www.chp.gov.hk/files/pdf/haamr_blood_key_findings2024.pdf
Publication: https://www.chp.gov.hk/en/static/109746.html
Rights notice: https://www.chp.gov.hk/en/static/24027.html

The source rights remain with the Government and relevant owners. No general open
licence for this adapted extract was identified in the checked CHP notice. That
notice expressly describes personal/internal non-commercial copies in complete,
unmodified form. The application MIT grant does not assign rights to source data.
This package carries extracted factual statistics and source coordinates; it does
not reproduce the PDF, figures, page layout or report prose.

Scope: 175 annual records, 2020–2024, two organisms. E. coli hospital-onset: 60;
K. pneumoniae community-onset: 55; K. pneumoniae hospital-onset: 60. The source
file does not provide E. coli community-onset rows in these key-findings tables.
Non-susceptibility remains the source metric; PHLSB R-only values are distinct.
Rounded patient counts, suppressed numerators, coverage flags and CI inequality
symbols are preserved. Percentages and CI are never recomputed from rounded counts.

Reproduce from locally retained original PDF and its acquisition JSON:
  python3 scripts/adapters/ha_blood.py
Fetch and validate an update before replacing usable data:
  python3 scripts/adapters/ha_blood.py --fetch
Python dependency: pdfplumber. Parser version and source SHA256 are in metadata.
