Hong Kong Flu Express surveillance

Source: Department of Health, Hong Kong SAR Government, via DATA.GOV.HK.
CSV: https://www.chp.gov.hk/files/misc/flux_data.csv
Dictionary: https://www.chp.gov.hk/files/pdf/flux_spec_en.pdf
Terms: https://data.gov.hk/en/terms-and-conditions
Original ownership remains with the Government and relevant organisations.
The application parser is project-owned MIT code; source data retain these terms.

This snapshot contains 669 weeks, 2014W1 to 2026W43, and all 31 source columns.
The first 2014 reporting week begins on 2013-12-29. The 27 indicator columns
retain their original units: counts, proportions on a 0–1 scale, consultation
or emergency rates per 1,000, and admission rates per 10,000 population.
Blank cells remain missing. No wastewater fields are present in this CSV.

The original CSV and dictionary remain at their official sources. Their hashes,
field definitions, units and processing version are in flu-metadata.json.
A dictionary revision requires review before the adapter replaces the snapshot.
