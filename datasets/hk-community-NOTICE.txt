Changes: the two public QC tables were parsed into a browser dataset and CSV.
The original cell text remains in the dataset raw_rows. Normalized values use
null for the source marker '/'. Seasons are read from the literal Sample ID
prefix. The original XLSX is provided unchanged.

Each QC table contains 189 identifiers. The paper separately reports 190
collected samples at 95 sites in 18 districts, and 185 samples in Figure 1.
Four QC identifiers have no Illumina metrics. No sample-level ARG abundance,
clinical AST counts or clinical pairing matrix is included in these QC tables.

The independently downloaded PRJNA1207805 ENA index contains 406 run records.
It has not been joined to the workbook identifiers: no verified crosswalk
was obtained, and archive dates extend beyond the paper's two campaigns.
ENA records are provided under the INSDC free and unrestricted data-record
access and redistribution policy, with appropriate citation.
https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ena/browser/view/PRJNA1207805
https://ena-browser-docs.readthedocs.io/en/latest/about/policies.html
