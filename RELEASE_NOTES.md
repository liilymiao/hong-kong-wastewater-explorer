# v1.6.0

Research query brings the question, data-source catalogue, time and study conditions, matching preview, exclusions and research decisions into one workflow. Queries can be saved and restored. Complete analysis links preserve page, language, selected years and week, forecast horizon, scoring, model visibility, replay timing and pattern settings. Source identity is checked before restoration; shared research conditions carry input-file fingerprints.

The COVID-19 research table filters complete monitoring weeks by dates and records missing or excluded weeks. Original observations and source fields are retained. The monitoring overview presents the research scope, current results and task entries before the trends; the introduction video remains in a collapsed section. The clinical indicator is defined as SARS-CoV-2 positivity among respiratory specimens received by PHLSB.

Three public research datasets are now loaded and queryable. PHLSB provides 234 annual outpatient records for 2020–2025, retaining reported percentages, organism totals, marker-positive counts and missing cells. The European sewage study provides 235 biological samples × 554 ResFinder genes (130,190 raw count cells), dated 2019–2021. The Hong Kong community sewage study provides 189 published sample identifiers and both supplementary QC tables. Its separate ENA archive index contains 406 runs with no verified crosswalk to those QC identifiers. Each dataset records its own source identity and terms.

Six further sources complete the nine-dataset research catalogue. Hospital Authority blood-culture tables contribute 175 annual records and wholesale supply contributes 444 sector/group records. BV-BRC contributes 15,777 experimental E. coli/ciprofloxacin AST records, with explicit genome links, duplicate/conflict flags and a 12,305-record conservative cohort. HKO supplies 2,830 recent daily rainfall records and Flu Express supplies 669 weeks across 27 indicators. Global Sewage 3 supplies all 17,402 reported count rows for a declared 16-sample subset of its fixed v1 release. Large browser assets load on demand.

Dataset filters and exports preserve source units and identifiers. PHLSB annual records support annual summaries; source totals remain distinct from verified drug-specific AST denominators. European raw gene counts are not normalized for abundance comparison. Hong Kong QC tables measure sequencing and assembly characteristics. The loaded sources contain no clinical–wastewater pairing matrix.

HA percentages and confidence intervals retain source precision; rounded or suppressed counts are not used to reconstruct exact rates. Supply uses DID with separate human and veterinary scope. BV-BRC retains experimental measurements, operators and methods; disk-diffusion/unit conflicts do not become MIC labels. Rainfall Trace remains a below-0.05 mm interval. Flu indicators retain their individual denominators. Global count samples join to verified ENA records; the unlinked Parquet geography table is kept separate.

Three local CSV inputs can create ecological tables: processed wastewater measurements, clinical counts, and explicit area correspondence. Clinical inputs require exact counts and experimental AST evidence. Conflicting duplicates, invalid denominators, incompatible units/methods/normalization and unsupported time resolution are reported. Environmental values use an unweighted arithmetic mean within each accepted indicator and clinical reporting period. Predictive feasibility remains unevaluated for these concurrent ecological rows.

Research ZIP exports include retained records, exclusions, matching issues, query conditions, source references and per-file hashes. Uploaded originals are preserved byte for byte. The COVID trend figure respects missing and excluded time gaps; saved forecast display columns follow the selected scoring mode. Forecasts are filtered by origin dates, while their original targets and frozen-model scope remain explicit. Spreadsheet-formula text is neutralized in generated CSVs; numerical cells and original uploaded files are preserved.

This release updates the website to **1.6.0**. The packaged Python application and separate course source ZIP remain **1.5.2**; scientific configuration remains **1.1**. Python source, saved COVID-19 results, frozen browser evidence, video and eight subtitle files are unchanged from the verified v1.5.2 release. The newly loaded research datasets have separate provenance in `research-datasets.json` and do not modify the frozen COVID-19 analysis. No models were rerun. The complete download includes source adapters, component source records and per-file checksums. [Data scope and update commands](DATA_SOURCES.md).

Historical data-availability reconstruction, sampling-regime sensitivity, new-period validation and user research remain pending. The recorded narration-provenance and course-format questions remain unchanged.
