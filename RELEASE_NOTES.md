# v1.6.1

Research tasks now lead directly to filters, results and downloads. Study notes follow the results, and source coverage, processing details and versions are grouped in the final collapsed **Data coverage & sources** section.

- Source-specific conditions are shared by the controls, summaries, links, saved query files and exports. Switching sources removes unrelated fields. Annual selections retain their exact years, including non-contiguous selections.
- PHLSB multi-drug queries show identified comparison series and coverage counts. Single-series summaries retain source percentage precision and show changes in percentage points. Year comparisons and derived changes can be downloaded.
- Weekly date filtering uses the complete reporting interval. A week that overlaps the selected dates remains included under the stated overlap rule.
- European ARG queries place sample coverage, gene previews and the full matrix download together. Hong Kong sample queries expose numerical sequencing and assembly QC metrics with sorting, range filters and group summaries.
- BV-BRC provides separate S/I/R cohort and MIC tasks, concise default columns, record details and explicit AST-record/genome counts.
- Local data matching starts with three visible upload cards. Uploaded clinical-area IDs drive the area filter, and original clinical reporting periods determine aggregation. Results show matched areas, reporting periods, contributing environmental samples and analysis rows.
- Dataset directories use current source metadata. Direct CSV and figure downloads sit alongside full research packages and saved queries.

Website version is **1.6.1**. All nine research-data snapshots, the Python **1.5.2** component, scientific configuration **1.1**, frozen COVID-19 results, video and eight subtitle tracks are preserved. No models were rerun and no new data snapshots were fetched for this release.
